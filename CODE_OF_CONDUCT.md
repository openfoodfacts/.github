# Code of Conduct

Open Food Facts is a non-profit, volunteer-driven open data and open source project. We are dedicated to providing a welcoming, respectful, and harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our Standards

- **No discrimination or harassment:** We do not tolerate harassment, discrimination, or threats of any kind in our repositories, issues, pull requests, discussions, Slack channels, or community events.
- **Respect volunteers' time and boundaries:** Most Open Food Facts contributors and maintainers are volunteers dedicating their free time. Please be considerate when asking questions, requesting reviews, or proposing changes, and respect answers such as "no", "not now", or "I don't know". Repeated or undue pressure on individual contributors is not acceptable.
- **Constructive collaboration:** Focus on constructive feedback, empathy, and shared goals. Respect maintainers' decisions on project scope and technical direction.
- **Large requests:** Requests likely to require significant effort or coordination should be sent to `contact@openfoodfacts.org` rather than directed at individual contributors.

## Reporting Abuse

If you experience or witness behavior that violates this Code of Conduct, please report it to **`abuse@openfoodfacts.org`** with as much detail and context as possible. Reports are handled confidentially by the Open Food Facts Association administrators.

## Enforcement

Violations of this Code of Conduct may result in a warning, temporary block, or permanent ban from Open Food Facts repositories and community spaces at the discretion of the Open Food Facts Association administrators.
