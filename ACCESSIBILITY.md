# Accessibility

Open Food Facts is committed to making our websites, mobile applications, open data tools, and documentation accessible to everyone, regardless of ability or assistive technology.

## Our Goals

- Follow recognized accessibility standards (such as [WCAG 2.1 AA](https://www.w3.org/WAI/standards-guidelines/wcag/)) across our web interfaces and mobile apps.
- Support keyboard navigation, screen readers, sufficient color contrast, and clear semantic markup in new UI components.
- Welcome accessibility improvements and audits from the community.

## Reporting Accessibility Barriers

If you encounter an accessibility barrier in any Open Food Facts repository or application:

1. Open an issue in the relevant repository describing:
   - What you were trying to do and where the barrier occurred (URL, screen, or component).
   - The assistive technology, browser, or OS you are using (if applicable).
   - Any suggestions for how to make it more accessible.
2. You can also reach out to the community on [Slack](https://slack.openfoodfacts.org/) or email `contact@openfoodfacts.org`.
